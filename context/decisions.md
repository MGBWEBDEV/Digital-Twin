| D24 | APPROVED | Fidelity axis. Both models share the same water balance, simulation time step, controller interface and per-pump calibrated flow rates. **Model 1** treats the rig as ideal: pumps start and stop instantly, the sensor switches exactly at its level, communication is instantaneous. **Model 2** = Model 1 plus the real-world effects that are measurable on the rig. Candidates: tube priming (water needed to fill the tubing before any reaches the syringe), run-on after OFF, the actual sensor trigger point and sensor reporting delay (including different switch-on/switch-off levels when draining), and communication/reaction delay (expected negligible). Inclusion rule: an effect enters Model 2 only if WP04 shows it larger than the syringe reading resolution (volumes) or the log timestamp resolution (times); otherwise it is documented as excluded. Not modelled unless data require it: head-dependent flow, random run-to-run noise (variability is measured and used for tolerances). Measurement rules: rig volumes are read visually after the water has settled; timing comes from host logs; on the rig, "overflow" means a settled level above a marked safe limit below capacity, and real overflow is only computed in the models, never caused physically; where a peak cannot be read as a settled level, the rig check is undetermined | Supersedes D08; narrows D13. The time-step size and equation form are deliberately not the fidelity difference (they can be mathematically equivalent). If no effect qualifies, the finding "Model 1 is sufficient at this rig's resolution" is recorded and a fallback is decided then; no effect is invented. Affects WP04, WP05, WP08, WP11 | 2026-11-25, researcher (via Claude Code session) |
